## 🚁 About

**Drone Shooter** is a fast-paced multiplayer drone combat game built with Unity and Photon PUN2. Engage in aerial dogfights with up to 8 players simultaneously, with real-time synchronization under 150ms latency. Use tilt-based controls to pilot your drone, navigate dynamic arenas, and outmaneuver opponents in intense 3D battles.

The project showcases robust network optimization, a custom lobby/matchmaking system, and responsive gameplay programming. It is designed as a showcase of multiplayer networking skills and game feel.

## ✨ Current Features

- 🎮 **Real‑time Multiplayer** – Supports 8+ concurrent players with <150ms latency via Photon PUN2
- 🕹️ **Tilt‑Based Controls** – Intuitive tilt steering (mobile/desktop) for precise drone maneuvering
- 🏟️ **Lobby & Matchmaking** – Custom system for creating/joining rooms and starting matches
- 💥 **Combat System** – Shoot, dodge, and outplay opponents in 3D arenas
- 📊 **Live HUD** – Health (100/100), match timer, and scoreboard
- ⚡ **Network Optimization** – Smooth interpolation, client-side prediction, and lag compensation

<br/>

## 🕹️ How to Play

**Controls**
- **WASD** (desktop) – move drone
- **Mouse ** – aim and look around
- **Click ** – fire weapons
- **Start** button – begin match when all players are ready

**Objective**  
Destroy enemy drones while keeping yours alive. The match timer counts down; the player with the highest score at the end wins. Use obstacles and speed to your advantage.
